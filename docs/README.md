# docs/

Design notes, research, and meeting records — anything that isn't code, hardware, or a final deliverable.

## Subfolders

- `fsm/` — finite state machine drafts and revisions (the canonical version lives here, exports go to `deliverables/`)
- `research/` — references, papers, vendor data, LCA sources for the three construction methods
- `meetings/` — meeting notes, dated `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`

## Conventions

- Markdown preferred. Diagrams as `.drawio`, `.excalidraw`, or `.svg` (track the source, not just a PNG export).
- Date your notes. Sign them with your name.
- If a research note ends in a decision that affects the build, copy the decision into the relevant subsystem README.
